BEFORE YOU ASK

A few things
worth knowing.

Still have a question?
We’re a message away.

Is basic counselling free?

Yes. Basic college selection and admission-process guidance is free. Confirm the scope of any separate service before agreeing to it.

Does a college listing mean you partner with it?

No. Directory colleges and institution partners are different. A partnership is labelled separately and does not guarantee admission or determine which college suits you.

Can you guarantee admission based on my rank?

No. Allotment depends on current eligibility rules, rank type, category, quota, choices, seat availability and the relevant authority’s process.

How should I use the fees and placement information?

Look for the source, academic year and verification date. Missing information stays unverified. Check the current official college documents before making a decision.
