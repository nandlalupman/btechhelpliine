# Student resources and college sources

The scholarship and state pages are curated discovery resources. They link students to the original authority; they do not submit applications, collect financial documents, decide eligibility or reproduce a competitor's complete content.

## Scholarship selection

The current selection includes PM-USP CSSS, AICTE Pragati/Saksham/Swanath, SC/ST higher-education support, PM-YASASVI Top Class College Education, Reliance Foundation's undergraduate programme and reviewed state-resource portals. Government schemes and the private foundation programme are labelled separately. A state portal entry can contain several schemes; it is not itself a promise of one award.

Primary starting points:

- [National Scholarship Portal](https://scholarships.gov.in/)
- [myScheme](https://www.myscheme.gov.in/)
- [Social Justice & Empowerment schemes](https://socialjustice.gov.in/index.php/schemes/101)
- [Ministry of Tribal Affairs scholarship information](https://tribal.nic.in/ScholarshiP.aspx)
- [Reliance Foundation undergraduate programme](https://www.scholarships.reliancefoundation.org/UG_Scholarship)

Use the current academic-year guidelines for benefit amounts, category/income rules, institution lists and overlapping-award rules. Fresh, renewal and institute-verification windows can differ. The site currently leaves deadlines unconfirmed and asks the student to check the provider's notice before recording a date in the tracker.

## State pages

All 28 states and 8 union territories have a page. The primary directory is [the Government of India website directory](https://igod.gov.in/sg/states); [India.gov.in's directory](https://www.india.gov.in/directory/web-directory) is another starting point. Government homepages are not always the application portals. Reviewed specialist portals are named where included. Where none has been added, the page says so and points to government/NSP/myScheme discovery.

Admission eligibility, a counselling quota and eligibility for a domicile-based benefit are separate questions. Listing colleges in a state does not establish that an applicant qualifies for any seat or scheme there.

## College evidence

- [MNIT B.Tech 2026-27 fee document](https://www.mnit.ac.in/cms/uploads/2026/05/Fee_UG_2026-27.pdf): semester tuition with category/income remission distinctions. Institute charges and living costs are additional.
- [MNIT's NIRF page](https://www.mnit.ac.in/about_us/nirf): the cited Engineering 2025 rank is historical institution-level information, independent of fees and placements.
- [JECRC University fee schedule](https://jecrcuniversity.edu.in/fee/): programme-specific tuition, other academic charges and one-time fees for 2026-27. A displayed total is not added again to its own components.
- [BITS first-degree fee schedule](https://www.bitsadmission.com/FD/FD_fee.html): specified entrant cohort, semester tuition, additional advances/charges and future-year changes. Pilani data is not silently applied to another campus.
- [SKIT admission FAQs](https://www.skit.ac.in/admissions/frequently-asked-queries.html): source-linked admission information; no numeric fee is inferred.
- [Poornima Group college prospectus](https://www.poornima.org/public/uploads/PGCProspectus2026.pdf): a source for Poornima College of Engineering. Poornima University's fees are not substituted.

Only the named field and its stated year/scope are covered by a source. An available ranking does not verify a placement figure; an official admission page does not verify every numeric field. Missing figures remain unverified. These are short editorial summaries and factual fields, not copied articles, brochures or downloaded campus-photo collections.

## Maintaining the content

1. Read the latest original notice, check the named institution/course/session and record the exact source URL.
2. Update only supported fields in the curated data. Preserve annual/semester/one-time distinctions, exclusions, refundable advances and conditions. Leave uncertain fields blank.
3. Update the review date only after checking the source. Re-run resource tests, regenerate the homepage, inspect the diff and sync college evidence if it changed.
4. Use the source-correction link on college pages, or the private message inbox, to review a student's suggestion. Confirm it with the authority before publication.

Reachability reports are local operational notes. A timeout, bot check or HTTP 200 error page is not evidence of an application opening or closing. No autonomous background scraping or periodic publishing job is enabled.
